## Grouped cross-validation

Two pre-registered 32-candidate grids were completed without using a locked
test:

1. The embedding dual-head family suppressed every learned long decision.
2. The hybrid character/word n-gram, embedding, and temporal-feature family
   improved the best pooled long result to 9/11 (81.82%). The candidate with
   the most accepted long decisions produced 12/17 (70.59%).

The required long precision is 98%, so no candidate was eligible for final
training. Thresholds were not weakened and the locked quality gates were not
run.
